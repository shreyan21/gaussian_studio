CAPTURE GUIDE

Capture quality controls 3D quality.

  1. Keep scene still. Move only the camera; do not rotate an object against a fixed background.
  2. Walk, do not pivot. Record 20-60 seconds or use 20-60 ordered views with 70-85% overlap.
  3. Lock capture. Keep zoom, focus, exposure, lighting, and distance consistent.
  4. Cover every surface. Unseen areas remain holes. Reflections, glass, blur, motion, and blank walls break stereo.
Bad captures are rejected.

If too few cameras register or dense fusion is too sparse, the job stops with capture advice instead of displaying scattered geometry.
