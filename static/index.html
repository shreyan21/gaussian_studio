QUICK GUIDE

From photographs to perspective.

  1. Capture overlapping views. Move around one stationary subject. Keep lighting, scale, and focus consistent.
  2. Keep upload order useful. Add neighboring camera positions in sequence. Auto-safe samples evenly if your GPU cannot use every upload together.
  3. Create, then explore. Drag to orbit. Right-drag or Shift-drag to pan. Scroll or use + / − to zoom.
  4. Export your result. The .ply contains Gaussian positions, scales, rotations, opacity, and colours.
AnySplat is native multi-view.

It jointly estimates camera poses and one Gaussian scene from uncalibrated inputs. More useful overlap can improve coverage; unrelated or blurry views can reduce quality.

AnySplat uses NVIDIA CUDA here. Depth Anything remains the one-image CPU fallback. Inference and viewing run locally after setup.
