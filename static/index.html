QUICK GUIDE

From photographs to perspective.

  1. Choose clear views. For an object, use front, back, left and right photographs with similar framing.
  2. Label every view. SHARP uses the labels to rotate its independent Gaussian predictions into one coordinate frame.
  3. Create, then explore. Drag in the viewport to orbit. Right-drag or Shift-drag to pan. Scroll or use + / − to zoom.
  4. Export your result. The .ply contains Gaussian positions, scales, rotations, opacity and colours.
Multi-view SHARP is geometric fusion.

Real views improve coverage, but SHARP does not natively reason across images. Unseen regions remain predictions, and seams or inconsistent geometry may remain.

SHARP weights are limited to non-commercial scientific research. Inference and viewing run locally.
